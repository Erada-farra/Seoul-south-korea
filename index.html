N SEOUL TOWER

A view above seoul

N Seoul Tower stands proudly atop Namsan Mountain, offering breathtaking panoramic views of Seoul. Originally built as a communication and observation tower, it has become one of the city's most iconic landmarks and a favorite destination for visitors.

The Han River

Flowing through the heart of Seoul, the Han River is more than a natural landmark—it is an essential part of the city's lifestyle. Its riverside parks, walking trails, and cycling paths provide a peaceful escape from the fast pace of urban life. By day, visitors enjoy outdoor activities and scenic views, while at night the river reflects the city's glowing skyline, creating one of Seoul's most memorable sights.
